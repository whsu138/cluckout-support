Time Tracking Basics

How do I track my time?

From iPhone:
1. Open CluckOut
2. Tap "Clock In" when you start working
3. Tap "Clock Out" when you finish

From Apple Watch (New in 1.4.3):
1. Open the CluckOut app on your Apple Watch
2. Tap "Clock In" or "Clock Out" directly from your wrist
3. Your status syncs automatically with your iPhone

New in 1.4.3: Full Apple Watch support allows you to track time from your wrist with real-time synchronization between devices. Perfect for quick clock in/out without reaching for your phone.

New in 1.5.4: The Clock screen shows Working or On Break status, a large hero timer, and side-by-side work duration with estimated pay. While on break, the hero shows break time and metrics show Work so far.

New in 1.5.0: Take a break while clocked in without stopping your session. Tap "Take a Break" to pause your work timer, then "Resume Work" when you're ready. Work duration automatically excludes break time for accurate earnings.

How do I take a break while clocked in?

1. Clock in as usual on the Clock tab
2. Tap "Take a Break" to start an open-ended pause
3. Your work timer stops and a live break timer shows elapsed break time
4. Tap "Resume Work" when you're ready to continue
5. Clock out when your session is complete—work duration excludes all break time

New in 1.5.0: Breaks end only when you tap "Resume Work." There are no preset break durations or automatic break-end alerts. Your earnings are based on work time, not total session length.

How do I add breaks to a manual time entry?

1. Go to the Summary tab and tap "+" to add an entry, or tap an existing entry to edit
2. Set your check-in and check-out times
3. In the Breaks section, add one or more break intervals with start and end times
4. The app shows total break time and work duration as you edit
5. Tap Save

New in 1.5.4: Moving check-in shifts breaks by the same offset; changing check-out clamps breaks into the session window. Validation banners call out break issues before you save.

New in 1.5.0: Break intervals must fall within your check-in/check-out window. Work duration is calculated as session time minus break time. Imported records with break totals show a read-only break summary when editing (no interval history).

1. Go to the Summary tab
2. Tap on the entry you want to edit
3. Modify the clock-in/clock-out times with second-level precision
4. Tap Save in the navigation bar

New in 1.5.4: Overlapping ranges are blocked with a clear banner. Duration and pay preview as you edit; rate fields show From category or Custom. Summary category breakdown and rows refresh immediately after you save.

• Each time entry can have its own hourly rate
• Set your default rate in Settings
• Modify individual entry rates by editing the entry
• Rates are preserved historically for completed entries

How are earnings calculated?

• Active entries use your current default rate
• Completed entries maintain their historical rates

What if notification permission is denied?

If notification permission is denied, the reminder toggle will be disabled and you'll see a warning message. To fix this:

1. Tap "Open Settings" in the Reminders section
2. Navigate to CluckOut in iOS Settings
3. Enable "Allow Notifications"
4. Return to the app and try enabling reminders again

New in 1.2.2: The app now provides clear guidance and direct access to iOS Settings when permission is denied.

Apple Watch

How do I use the Apple Watch app?

New in 1.4.3: CluckOut now includes a full Apple Watch app for time tracking on the go!

1. Make sure your Apple Watch is paired with your iPhone
2. Install CluckOut on your iPhone (the watch app installs automatically)
3. Open the CluckOut app on your Apple Watch
4. Tap "Clock In" or "Clock Out" directly from your wrist
5. Your status syncs automatically with your iPhone in real-time

Features: Real-time duration display, connection status indicator, automatic synchronization, and error handling with user-friendly messages.

New in 1.5.2: Add CluckOut to your Apple Watch Smart Stack for quick access. Tap the widget (rectangular or circular) to open the Watch app—ideal for clocking in, taking a break, or checking your session. Requires watchOS 10 or later.

New in 1.5.0: Take a break and resume work directly from your Apple Watch while checked in. The watch shows on-break status (orange), work duration, and live break time synced with your iPhone.

How do I add CluckOut to the Apple Watch Smart Stack?

1. On your Apple Watch, open the Smart Stack (turn the Digital Crown up from the watch face)
2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Edit Widgets (or long-press a widget and choose Edit Widgets)
3. Tap the + button and select CluckOut
4. Choose a rectangular or circular layout
5. Tap Done—then tap the widget anytime to open CluckOut

New in 1.5.2: The Smart Stack widget launches the Watch app with one tap. It does not show live status or timers—use the Watch app itself for clock in/out and breaks. Requires watchOS 10 or later.

How do I take a break from Apple Watch?

1. Clock in on your iPhone or Apple Watch
2. While checked in, tap "Take a Break" on your Apple Watch
3. The watch shows on-break status, work duration, and live break time
4. Tap "Resume Work" on your watch when you're ready to continue
5. Clock out when your session is complete

New in 1.5.0: Break controls sync in real time between iPhone and Apple Watch. Work duration on the watch reflects time minus breaks, matching your iPhone.

What Apple Watch models are supported?

The CluckOut Watch app requires Apple Watch running watchOS 8.0 or later (restored in 1.5.4). The Smart Stack widget still requires watchOS 10.0 or later. The watch app requires a paired iPhone with the CluckOut app installed.

Why isn't my watch app connecting to my iPhone?

If you see a connection error on your watch app:

• Make sure your Apple Watch is paired with your iPhone
• Ensure both devices are nearby and Bluetooth is enabled
• Try restarting both the watch app and iPhone app
• Check that the CluckOut app is installed on your iPhone

The watch app requires an active Watch Connectivity session to communicate with your iPhone. The connection status indicator shows whether the session is active.

Can I use the watch app without my iPhone nearby?

The watch app requires an active connection to your iPhone via Watch Connectivity to clock in and out. While you can view the app interface, clock in/out operations require the iPhone to be nearby and connected via Bluetooth.
